# Record

Not design. This is why things were ruled out, what was learned the hard way,
and what is currently only a guess. Consult it before re-proposing something.

Nothing here is a decision. Decisions live in DESIGN.md, and there is one.

## Findings

Non-obvious, expensive to rediscover.

- Time poverty is arithmetic, not duration. An hour must visibly cost a
  different hour.
- The interesting part of work is the getting hired, not the labor.
- A system a player cannot tell is running does not belong.
- Corruption is only legible against a stable truth. A corrupted sole channel is
  indistinguishable from a broken build.
- A wrong sign that reads like a sign gets acted on. One that reads broken gets
  discounted.
- Friend-slop has no single-player core to prove. Contradicts standard advice.
- Emergence is few verbs times many systems, not many features. Lethal Company
  has about six verbs.
- Lethal Company has no sabotage mechanic. Leaving people behind falls out of a
  shared objective, individual mortality, and a door that closes on a schedule.
- Realism is an excellent generator and a bad architect. Keep real systems whose
  failure involves another player; drop the ones whose failure is private.
  Losing a place in line qualifies. Filling out a form correctly does not.
- Fidelity to the real condition creates an obligation to be accurate about
  misery, which is pressure on Pillar 1. Maximally real institutions plus a
  deliberately abstract player situation puts the absurdity on the office and
  never on the person.
- Comedy requires witnessing, which requires co-presence.

## Rejected

Reopen by attacking the reason, not by re-proposing the idea.

- **Blank slate as a method.** Correct about how the output should feel, wrong
  about process. Every generous-feeling game of this kind is tightly authored
  around a tiny verb set.
- **Designed sabotage verbs.** A betrayal button is legible, accusable, and
  socially banned within two sessions.
- **Waiting as duration.** Minutes of nothing in order to progress. The thing
  worth buying is commitment, not duration.
- **Fine balance.** Cannot coexist with many viable playstyles.
- **Slots instead of a clock.** Three abstract slots per day, scale-invariant by
  construction. Rejected because time, day, and night are real things in this
  game and abstracting them away to dodge one unknown constant is the wrong
  trade. Replaced by a real clock with a deferred conversion constant, which is
  scale-invariant for the same reason without costing the diegesis.
- **Measuring day length by walking the current city.** The city has no alleys or
  districts and blocks are not yet distinguishable, so any number measured now is
  wrong and would be designed against.
- **A mechanic that exists to prove a point.** Makes this a Serious Empathy
  Game, which gets respectful reviews and zero players.

## Constraints on future decisions

Cheap to violate by accident, expensive to notice.

- **No ledger, anywhere.** Deniability dies the moment a UI shows who did what.
  Applies especially to compliance or violation trackers.
- **Rules known up front, enforcement uncertain.** A known rule you choose to
  break is a gamble and a story. An unknown rule that bites reads as a broken
  game.
- **Failure must be public, legible, narratable, and survivable.** "I got on the
  wrong bus" is a story. "A hidden stat crossed a threshold" is not. Instant
  death ends the bit.
- **Many ways to lose and many ways to sabotage require separable outcomes.**
  Under a single shared outcome, sabotage is either griefing or misnamed
  cooperation. Forced by the objective, not chosen.
- **Costs must be non-substitutable.** Work, scavenging, hustling players, and
  gaming an institution are one strategy in four costumes if they all reduce to
  time in, money out.
- **Starts should gate verbs, not scale numbers.** 20% slower is a difficulty
  setting. Removing a channel is a different game.
- **Some runs are unwinnable.** Acceptable only in combination with a short run.

## Hypotheses

Guesses. Listed under the question they would answer if true.

### Q1, the win

Mostly promoted to DESIGN.md. Still hypothetical:

- Whether being housed first is good or costly is the housed player's choice,
  not the designer's. Requires both options to hurt.
- Being housed subtracts verbs, so the leader is the most constrained player and
  last place is the freest.
- Residential rules supply a defensible no, unverifiable by the others.
- Pooling forced by market geometry rather than encouraged, making roommate
  selection the negotiation.
- Rest is where the negotiation lives, because it recurs.
- No end-of-run grade. Consequences land during the run instead, so the four
  players decide what 3/4 meant rather than the game telling them. Depends on
  whether anything persists between runs.

### Q2, the pressure

- Institutions are the antagonist rather than a clock. A clock cannot be blamed.
- Bureaucracy splits the party as a side effect of being modeled accurately.
- Appointments rather than queues.
- A held place in line is an object, not a timer.
- Inspections as the enforcement surface for residential rules.
- Tiredness corrupts rather than slows.

### Q3, the verbs

- The core five, from an earlier pass: a discoverable price varying by area; a
  deadline known at minute zero, random per run; somewhere to earn; somewhere to
  stop, where sleep quality sets tomorrow; other players who can help or lie.
- Physical carryable objects and player-to-player handoff, since without a
  transfer layer the only cooperative verb is talking.

## Resolved contradictions

- Findings says a system a player cannot tell is running does not belong; an
  earlier pass pinned tiredness to having no visible number. Resolved: legible
  and quantified are different requirements. See DESIGN.md.

## Parked

Liked, not load-bearing.

- Client-side corruption at draw time, so two players read a sign differently
  and both report honestly.
- Booze clears symptoms briefly while real tiredness climbs faster.
- *You Have a Dog*: subtracts and grants at once. Under residential rules it
  makes a player both harder to house and harder to host.
- An NPC who agrees to hold a place in line and later denies the agreement.
- NPCs who cut lines, requiring confrontation.
- Pairs rather than solos as the split unit.
- Driving. Undecidable before Q1, since whether a car trivializes the map
  depends on what winning requires.

## Corrections to earlier claims

Kept because both were used as arguments and both were wrong.

- Lethal Company's quota is not purely binary. Surplus credits carry, buying
  ship upgrades, tools, and access to harder moons, so it does reward win
  quality, in forward-looking resources rather than a displayed grade. This is a
  third option between grading and not grading.
- A car does not negate addiction. Addiction's cost is a recurring want another
  player can supply, withhold, or lie about supplying; a car only makes the
  errand cheaper. The pair is strong, not degenerate. Relevant because "some
  pairs are overpowered" is the argument that leads back to point weighting.

## Pool curation

Do not weight entries. Curate pairs later, by eye, once hands have been watched:
a small blocklist or a few hand-authored pairings. A tier list is a ledger and
invites the argument that the dealer got it wrong.

Entries considered so far, against the qualification test:

- **Broken-down car.** Strong bad-or-good hybrid. An object with value to
  everyone, fixable, borrowable, deniable out loud.
- **Debt.** Qualifies. A number another player can pay or promise to pay and
  then not. Also closes the lease chain, which is now load-bearing.
- **Addiction.** Qualifies structurally, not thematically: a recurring want with
  a schedule that another player can supply, withhold, or lie about.
- **Illiteracy.** Qualifies, and creates dependence on another player to read.
  Its cost is that it tethers you to someone, which fights separation. Dead
  weight only if dealt alone, which two-pool dealing prevents.
- **Psychiatric conditions.** Weakest candidate. Mostly private experience,
  corrupted perception with nothing to check it against, and no handle for the
  other three. Nothing to trade, nothing to witness.
